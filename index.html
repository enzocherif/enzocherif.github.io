Simulation

ABS & EBD braking system

With Ewen Guivarch

Stop a car at 100 km/h as fast as possible without locking the wheels.

4ABS controllers designed and compared

  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • Control
ReportPDF, 4.4 MB

What I did

Modelled the wheel and vehicle dynamics in MATLAB/Simulink, then designed and compared four ABS controllers (continuous PI, discrete PI, on-off and an improved on-off) and added EBD front/rear brake distribution.

Result

The improved on-off controller gave the shortest stopping distances, with wheel slip held close to the optimal 0.2. EBD prevented the rear wheels from locking early.

Internship in Thailand — TFII, Bangkok

Research internshipApril – August 2024

Programming for modern microcontrollers

Thai-French Innovation Institute (TFII), King Mongkut’s University of Technology North Bangkok

  • 17weeks in Bangkok
  • 10lab guides written in French and English
  • 24/24criteria rated “Excellent” by my supervisor
  • 1presentation to a French Ministry delegation

I worked on the energy management of a solar electric vehicle: a control system that makes the best use of variable solar power while keeping the vehicle stable.

Everything ran on a Texas Instruments TMS320F28335 board: generating PWM signals, reading voltage and current sensors through the ADC, and driving buck and boost converters.

Research

  • Studied and optimised the voltage and current transducers (LEM sensors) used to measure high voltages and currents.
  • Designed test protocols and ran experiments on the lab’s power benches.

Teaching

  • Wrote ten lab guides, first in French then in English, and tested each one myself.
  • Taught a small group of Thai students, who ended up building a working boost converter.

Power electronics

  • PWM generation and duty-cycle regulation on the F28335.
  • Buck and boost DC-DC converters, from theory to optimisation, with MATLAB for development.

May 29, 2024

After only a month at TFII, I presented my work on the F28335 board and the sensors to a delegation from the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs, led by Mr. Matthieu Peyraud, during their visit to the institute.
